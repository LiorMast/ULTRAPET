# Hero photos

The photos of the home page hero live in this folder. **Drop image files here and they appear on the site**
— no code or settings to change.

- Formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
- Order: by file name. Number them to control it: `01-dog.jpg`, `02-cat.jpg`, `03-...`.
- Every photo is shown for 7 seconds, with a gentle crossfade and a very slow zoom.
- 0 photos: the hero shows a plain dark background. 1 photo: it stays still. 2 or more: slideshow.
- Size: wide (landscape) photos at least 1920px across work best. Bigger files are fine: the build resizes each
  photo to 960px and 1920px WebP files automatically.
- The left side (right side in Hebrew) of the photo sits under a dark gradient with the headline, so keep the
  subject near the middle or the opposite side.
- The photos are decorative, so they have no alt text. Don't put text inside them.

The three photos here now are placeholders from the old site (Pexels, free for commercial use). Replace or delete
them any time.
